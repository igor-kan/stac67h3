STAC67H3: Regression Analysis

University of Toronto Scarborough · Fall 2026

Comprehensive course portal featuring the official course schedule, analytical lecture derivations, R Markdown case studies, interactive statistical simulations, and mathematical reference toolkits.

📊 Lecture Notes & Schedule

Course schedule, Gauss-Markov theorem, hat matrix \(\mathbf{H}\), geometry of least squares, multicollinearity, and regularized regression (Ridge / LASSO).

✏️ Problem Sets & Projects

Assignments A1–A3, tutorial quizzes (Q1–Q3), and complete guidelines for the R Markdown Case Study Project.

💻 Code & Simulations

Interactive in-browser statistical simulations (dynamic OLS, leverage sensitivity, Cook’s distance), R ggplot2 diagnostics, and Python models.

📐 Reference & Math

Matrix algebra theorems (idempotent matrices, quadratic forms), ANOVA identities, R & R Markdown cheat sheets, and course bibliography.
